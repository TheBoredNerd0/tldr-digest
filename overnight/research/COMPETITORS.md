# Competitor matrix

| Product | Shape | Strong at | Users complain / weak at | We can beat it by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TLDR (tldr.tech)** | Email newsletter + web archive per edition | Concise 2–3 sentence summaries; clear issue titles | 14 separate editions; archive is per-edition only | One page across all 14 editions + 16 other outlets, deduped (already done) |
| **Techmeme** | Text river, clustered | Density, clustering of outlets per story, no images | Dated look; tech only | Keep density via a compact mode; add topic tiles + world/SG news |
| **AIBriefs** (Show HN, Sep 2026) | Card feed, AI summaries | Saved, ⌘K search, density toggle, share | HN: "Claude slop UI" look; wants dense/brutalist mode, RSS, auto-update, spacebar/keyboard paging | Ship compact mode + keyboard nav + RSS without the generic pastel-chip aesthetic |
| **KBlip** | Bulleted one-liners per category | Extremely skimmable | No images, no blurbs | Compact list mode gives the same skim speed |
| **Kagi News (Kite)** | Static app over daily JSON, once a day | Calm "news diet", open source, keyword filters | Few stories by design | We already have breadth; add archive + cross-day search they lack |
| **Google News / Flipboard** | Personalized, image-led | Polish, personalization | Endless scroll, tracking | No tracking, finite daily page, offline-friendly static HTML |
| **Feedly / Inoreader** | RSS readers | Source control, read state, saved | Overwhelming unread counts, paid AI | Read-state + saved without accounts |

Shared gaps in *this* site today: broken archive order, no day-to-day navigation,
no cross-day search, no saved list, no compact view, no RSS out, no keyboard nav.
