# Design patterns worth adopting (ranked by frequency × evidence)

1. **Newest-first archive grouped by period** (TLDR archives, ui-patterns, every blog) — fixes our P0.
2. **Density toggle: cards ↔ compact list** (AIBriefs, HN feedback ×3, NN/g: lists scan better).
3. **Saved / bookmarks** (AIBriefs, Feedly, Inoreader, CN news list designs' "收藏" tab).
4. **Global search with a keyboard shortcut** (AIBriefs Ctrl+K, Linear-style; HN asked for keyboard paging).
5. **Prev / next issue navigation** (every newsletter archive; NN/g: never dead-end a page).
6. **RSS out** (HN feedback on AIBriefs; Techmeme, TLDR all offer it).
7. **Front-page "today" lead** (NN/g homepage = newspaper front page; Kite "once a day").
8. **Chunk headings for layer-cake scanning** (NN/g) — we have topic sections; keep headings strong.
9. **Avoid the generic AI-generated look** (HN critique): system font stack, restrained palette,
   no pastel dot-chips everywhere; let article images carry the colour.
10. **Respect reduced motion, visible focus, AA contrast** (WCAG 2.2) — baseline hygiene.
