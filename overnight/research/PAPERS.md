# Research & studies

| Source | Takeaway | Applies here |
|---|---|---|
| Nielsen, *How Users Read on the Web* (NN/g) | 79% scan; scannable layout +47%, concise +58% usability | Compact list mode; keep blurbs short |

| NN/g, *First 2 Words* | First ~11 characters of a headline drive selection | Don't prefix headlines with source names (we badge the source separately) |
| *Factuality Checking in News Headlines with Eye Tracking* (arXiv 2006.09736) | Headlines get disproportionate attention | Headline typography is the main lever |
| *The Power of Framing: How News Headlines Guide Search Behavior* (arXiv 2508.17131) | Headline framing shapes later searching | Cross-day search is a natural follow-up action |
