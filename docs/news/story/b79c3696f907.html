NEA issues legal notices under haze law to 10 Singapore-based companies, including Asia Pulp & Paper

First reported 21:00 SGT, 23 Oct 2026 · 3 sources

Singapore's National Environment Agency issued legal notices under its haze law to 10 Singapore-based companies, including Asia Pulp & Paper and Wilmar. The firms are asked to provide more documents on their links to fires in Indonesia.

Why it matters

Marks the first use of Singapore's haze law against these palm oil and forestry firms.
