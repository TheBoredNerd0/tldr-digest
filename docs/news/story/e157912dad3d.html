Bank of Korea warns country's chip exports could fall by up to $100 billion if AI spending slows

First reported 10:17 SGT, 27 Oct 2026 · 2 sources

South Korea's central bank says chip exports could fall $50 billion to $100 billion below earlier forecasts if US tech giants slow AI capital spending next year. Chips make up roughly half of the country's exports.

Why it matters

Chips are about half of South Korea's exports; a slowdown could trim 0.4 percentage points off GDP growth.
