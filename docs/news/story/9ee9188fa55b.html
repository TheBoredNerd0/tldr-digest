Microsoft patches a severe flaw in its Android Authenticator app that could let untrusted apps impersonate Microsoft apps and obtain delegated account tokens

First reported 12:42 SGT, 27 Oct 2026 · 2 sources

Microsoft patched CVE-2026-80097 (CVSS 8.6) in Authenticator for Android, which could let apps with no declared permissions impersonate Microsoft apps and obtain work account access tokens. Microsoft classified it as a local elevation-of-privilege flaw.

Why it matters

A flaw that could expose work account tokens on Android devices is relevant to anyone tracking CVEs and mobile security.
