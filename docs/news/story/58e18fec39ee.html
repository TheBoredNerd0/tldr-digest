Five listed directorships cap among proposed changes to corporate governance code, SGX rules

First reported 17:30 SGT, 27 Oct 2026 · 2 sources

Singapore's CGAC and SGX RegCo propose changes to the corporate governance code and listing rules, including a cap that could limit directors to five directorships in Singapore-listed companies. Key board committees may need a majority of independent directors.

Why it matters

Could affect board independence and how many listed boards each director can sit on, which bears on investor confidence.
