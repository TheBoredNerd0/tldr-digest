    Several Houston residents interviewed in person, including a business owner of 22 years and an energy-sector entrepreneur, with no net worth stated by them. Their advice: 1. Find a business that pays you recurring income every month, instead of chasing one-time sales. 2. Save money early, since compound interest is the greatest force in the universe. 3. Figure out what you are good at and do that, rather than following your passion. 4. Do not walk into a room asking what they can do for you; ask how you can serve them. 5. Pay attention to red flags instead of ignoring them because you want something so badly.
